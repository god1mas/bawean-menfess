# Indonesian legal/PSE review checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice or a compliance conclusion.

- Identify the legal operator and required public identity/contact disclosures.
- Determine PSE Lingkup Privat registration applicability and status.
- Confirm lawful bases, notices, consent wording, data-subject request handling,
  deletion/preservation duties, and breach-notification procedure.
- Review treatment of children, sensitive personal data, and harmful allegations.
- Review cross-border processing by Vercel, Supabase, and other processors.
- Confirm UGC governance, accessible reporting, complaint response, takedown,
  law-enforcement request, and user-notification duties.
- Confirm retention periods and lawful-hold process.
- Review intellectual-property complaint handling.
- Record counsel, scope, date, conclusions, and required remediation before
  approving public launch.
